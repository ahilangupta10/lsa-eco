  • ✅ Real-time AI coach that knows your name, score and streak
  • ✅ 4-level leaderboard: City → District → Zone → Nation
  • ✅ Daily challenges that reset & reward consistent action
  • ✅ Gamified badge progression — real motivation to keep going
